A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
A clearer picture.

Still curious about something?
Ask the Arcko team ↗

What ages do you teach?+

We welcome children ages 4–14 across three stages: Young Creators (4–7), Design Builders (7–10) and Creative Innovators (10–14). Suitable ages vary by programme. We can help you choose a class that fits your child’s readiness and interests.

How big are the classes?+

Our classes have a maximum of 8 children, giving each child space to participate and receive guidance. Contact us for the current course schedule.

Do you teach in English or Thai?+

Our classes are delivered bilingually in English and Thai, so children can follow, contribute and feel comfortable as they learn.

Are there tests or grades?+

There are no tests or grades. We follow each child’s progress through teacher observations, a photographed portfolio, an end-of-term showcase and an individual written report.

My child is not “the creative one.” Is this still for them?+

Yes. Children do not need to be confident artists to begin. Our classes invite them to ask questions, explore possibilities and work through ideas at their own pace. Curiosity is a wonderful starting point.
