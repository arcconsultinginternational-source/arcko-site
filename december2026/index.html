Who is this camp for?

Children aged 4–10 who enjoy exploring, making and discovering. Tell us your child’s age on LINE so we can discuss the right fit.

What are the dates and hours?

Week 1 is 14–18 December 2026. Week 2 is 21–25 December 2026. Each week runs Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, in Sathorn, Bangkok.

Can we join for just one day?

Yes. The early-bird day rate is ฿1,390 per child. Select “One day” above and choose your date. Our team will confirm the activity schedule and availability.

Can we combine the discounts?

There is a 5% offer for two weeks and a 5% offer for siblings or besties. The estimate applies one 5% discount at a time. Our team will confirm which offers apply to your booking on LINE.

How do we reserve and pay?

Choose dates and the number of children, review your total, then pay using the QR. Tap Confirm via LINE and attach your slip. Our team verifies payment and confirms availability and your place.

What should my child bring? What if plans change?

Before paying, please ask our team for the packing list, any food or material arrangements, and cancellation or rescheduling terms. Share any allergies or access needs directly with our team.
